Method and caveats

Disclaimer. Maintained by Soren Dayton (soren@thefai.org). This is an evolving product built from open-source research; nothing in it has been validated against primary records, and I do not vouch for the data. Use it as a starting point, not a citation.

Who is in. Every district where the Wikipedia results table (live, 2026-11-06) does not show the incumbent re-elected, plus the two nonvoting delegate seats that opened. That yields called winners, leaders and trailers in uncalled races, and the two Louisiana runoffs. Six returning former members (Carl, Solis, Bean, Allred, Luria, McAdams) are included and flagged; they are new to the 120th Congress but not true freshmen. Incumbents who merely changed districts (Casar, Wasserman Schultz, Kennedy, Menefee) are excluded, as is CA-40 (two incumbents). Trailers more than 3 points back (Vang CA-7, Hernandez CA-14, Hill AK-AL) are omitted.

Legislative service and committees. From each member's Ballotpedia page (officeholder history and "Committee assignments" section) cross-checked against Wikipedia. Committee lists show the most recent session first. Tribal and territorial legislatures count. Leadership roles come from Ballotpedia committee listings plus a verification search for each chamber-level claim (sources in the archive). Where Ballotpedia has no committee section (older service, e.g. Biss, Ojeda, McAdams's state Senate), the row says so.

Caucus predictions.

  • Evidence hierarchy. Ideological caucus tags are predicted from, in descending order of weight: the caucus-affiliated PAC's own endorsement list; prior membership; self-identification (for example, DSA membership); and finally district and biography.
  • Endorsement lists used. NewDem Action Fund (50 endorsements); Blue Dog PAC's 2026 list; Freedom Caucus Fund; Republican Main Street PAC; Congressional Progressive Caucus PAC; Justice Democrats.
  • Republican Study Committee. Treated as the default for any Republican without a contrary signal.
  • Republican Governance Group (RG2). Publishes no endorsement list and no membership list. Its PAC (FEC C00433060) gave only to incumbents, leadership PACs and one losing primary candidate (Robert Smullen, NY-21) in the 2026 cycle, so there is no direct RGG signal for any freshman. Every RGG tag is a guess, marked Low, and should not be read as evidence of membership, recruitment, or any communication from the group.
  • RG2 context. Our estimate is that roughly 15 of the group's approximately 41 members who sought re-election lost, among them Valadao (chair) and Young Kim (vice chair, who lost a member-on-member race to Ken Calvert), and that another half-dozen held on by under four points. The group enters the 120th Congress badly depleted and will rebuild from survivors rather than from this freshman class.
  • Identity and affinity caucuses. CBC, CHC, CAPAC, Native American, Equality, veterans, labor and law-enforcement caucuses are inferred from reported background and listed separately from the ideological tags.
  • Timing. Nobody has joined anything yet. Freshmen typically announce caucus memberships in December and January.

Known gaps. Bios are thin for several open-seat Republicans in Florida and Texas (Rogers, Strada, Gruters, Schwartzel, Bonck) and for Dembo (KY-6). Vote margins in uncalled races come from a third-party tracker dated 11/06 and from local reporting; confirm against AP before relying on them. The NC-5, MN-1, NC-3 and NY-21 margins are inside recount range.
